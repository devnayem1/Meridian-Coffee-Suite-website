Our story

Started with one cart.

Wayfarer started in 2020 with a converted espresso cart and a map of Brooklyn's weekend markets. The idea was simple: bring specialty coffee to people who weren't looking for it — and let the quality do the talking.

Today we run three carts, a full event booking service, and a small roastery in Red Hook. But the cart is still where we started, and where we still feel most like ourselves.
