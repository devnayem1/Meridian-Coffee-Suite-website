From the kitchen

Everything made before you wake.

The alarm goes at 4:15. By 5:30 the ovens are on and the lamination is already done — we start the croissant dough the night before, always. There's no shortcut that doesn't taste like one.

We source our flour from a small mill in Vermont, our butter from Normandy, and our chocolate from a single-origin cacao cooperative in Ecuador. The ingredient list on our croissant is four lines long. That's the point.

Saoirse Keane
Head Baker · 7 years
