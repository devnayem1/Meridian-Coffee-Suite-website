Section 02Your account

To place an order or subscribe, you may need to create an account. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials safe and for all activity that happens under your account.

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account or place an order.
  • The information you provide must be accurate and current.
  • You are responsible for keeping your password secure. We recommend a unique, strong password.
  • If you suspect unauthorized use of your account, contact us immediately at security@meridiancoffee.com.

Section 03Orders and payment

When you place an order, you are making an offer to purchase. We reserve the right to accept or decline any order. We will charge your payment method when your order is confirmed. All prices are in US dollars and exclude applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

If we are unable to fulfil your order — for example, if a bean is sold out — we will notify you and refund any payment within 5 working days.

We never store your full payment card details on our servers. Payments are processed by our payment provider, who handles your card information directly under PCI-DSS compliance.

Section 04Subscriptions

If you subscribe to our coffee subscription, you are agreeing to recurring deliveries at the cadence you select (weekly, fortnightly, or monthly). You will be charged at the start of each billing cycle.

You can pause, change cadence, or cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard, or by emailing subscriptions@meridiancoffee.com. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle.

Section 05Shipping and delivery

We ship within the United States. Free standard shipping on orders over $40. We aim to ship within 48 hours of roasting; transit time depends on your address. We are not responsible for delays caused by carriers, weather, or other circumstances outside our control.

If your package is lost or damaged in transit, contact us within 14 days of the expected delivery date and we will work with the carrier to resolve it.

Section 06Returns and refunds

Coffee is a perishable product, so we do not accept returns of opened bags. If your coffee arrives damaged, stale, or not what you ordered, write to us within 14 days at support@meridiancoffee.com and we will replace or refund it. We honour our roast quality without question.

For other items (equipment, gift cards), please see the specific return policy at the time of purchase.

Questions about privacy?

Our privacy policy covers what we collect, how we use it, and how you can control it. It is in plain English.

Effective April 1, 2026 Last updated March 28, 2026

Section 01Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Meridian Coffee Roasters LLC ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website, store, subscription program, and related services.

We believe in transparent, plain-language privacy. This policy is meant to be readable. If anything is unclear, write to us at privacy@meridiancoffee.com.

Short version: we collect what we need to run our business, never sell your data, and let you delete your account at any time. The full policy below covers the details.

Section 02What we collect

We collect information you give us directly, and a small amount we collect automatically.

Information you provide

  • Account information: name, email address, password (stored hashed).
  • Order information: shipping address, billing address, phone number (optional).
  • Payment information: handled by our payment provider; we never see or store your card numbers.
  • Preferences: taste profile from the quiz, brewing equipment, subscription cadence.
  • Communications: emails or messages you send us.

Information we collect automatically

  • Device data: browser type, operating system, screen size — used to make the site display correctly.
  • Usage data: pages you visit, time on site, referrer — used in aggregate to improve the site.
  • Cookies: small files that remember your cart and preferences. See our Cookie section below.

Section 03How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Process your orders and ship your coffee.
  • Run your subscription and send delivery updates.
  • Reply to your messages and provide support.
  • Send you transactional emails (order confirmation, shipping, account changes).
  • Send you our newsletter (only if you have opted in — and you can opt out anytime).
  • Improve the website and our products in aggregate, anonymous form.
  • Comply with legal obligations such as tax and accounting.

We never sell your personal data to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers.

Section 04Who we share data with

We share data with a small number of service providers, only to the extent necessary to run our services:

  • Payment processor: to handle your card transactions securely.
  • Shipping carriers: name and address only, to deliver your order.
  • Email delivery service: to send transactional and (opt-in) marketing emails.
  • Analytics provider: aggregated, anonymized usage data only.
  • Legal requirements: if required by valid legal process or to protect our rights.

All providers are bound by data processing agreements that limit their use of your data to providing the service to us.

Section 05Cookies and tracking

We use cookies for essential functionality (cart, login session, preferences) and for limited analytics. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may break functionality such as the cart or staying logged in.

Section 06Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct any inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and your associated data.
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time, via the unsubscribe link.
  • Object to certain types of processing.

To exercise any of these rights, log in to your account or write to privacy@meridiancoffee.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Section 07Data retention

We keep your data only as long as necessary:

  • Active accounts: retained while your account is open.
  • Closed accounts: personal data deleted within 90 days of closure.
  • Order history: retained for 7 years for tax and accounting compliance.
  • Newsletter: retained until you unsubscribe.

Section 08Security and contact

We protect your data with industry-standard measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all traffic, encrypted storage of sensitive fields, hashed passwords, regular security reviews, and limited access for our team. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to keep yours safe.

If you suspect a security issue, contact us immediately at security@meridiancoffee.com.
