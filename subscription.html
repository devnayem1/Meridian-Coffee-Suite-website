How we source

The bean has a face and a name.

We visit every farm we buy from. Not once — every year. Our sourcing team makes two origin trips annually and maintains direct relationships with 26 farming families across Ethiopia, Colombia, Costa Rica, and Guatemala.

Direct trade means the farmer earns more. It also means we know exactly what we're sending you — because we were there when it was picked.

Direct trade
No middlemen. Farmers earn 40–80% above commodity price.
Roasted to order
We roast your bag the day we receive your order. Not before.
Full traceability
Every bag includes farm, lot, harvest date, and process details.
Compostable packaging
Certified home-compostable bags. Box is recycled cardboard.
