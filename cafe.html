The space

Eighteen seats. No distractions.

Slow Bar opened in 2021 inside a converted ironmonger's shop on East 7th. We kept the brick walls and the silence. We added a twelve-foot cypress bar and a water system most hospitals would envy.

Eighteen seats. No music after 10am. No laptops on weekends. Just the sound of water at 93 degrees and coffee doing what it does best.

Pour-over to order
Every filter coffee brewed individually. No batch brew, ever.
No rush, ever
Your cup takes as long as it takes. Seats are held for 90 minutes.
Farm-direct sourcing
Every bean on our menu has a face, a name, and a story we can tell.
